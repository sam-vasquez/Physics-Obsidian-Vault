Not to be confused with [[Linear Response]]. There's surely a better name for this page.

In thermodynamics, a *response function* is any variable that responds to applied work.

Examples from [[Internal Energy (Mechanical System)|mechanical systems]]:

[[Compressibility]] is the response of volume to pressure.
$$
k_T = -\frac{1}{V} \left( \frac{\partial V}{\partial p} \right)_T = -\frac{1}{V} \left( \frac{\partial^2 G}{\partial p^2 } \right)_T.
$$
[[Heat Capacity]] is the response of heat to temperature.


Examples from [[Internal Energy (Magnetic System)|magnetic systems]] (using $dE = TdS + HdM$):

[[Magnetic Susceptibility]] is the response of magnetization to an external magnetic field.
$$
\chi_0 = \left( \frac{\partial M}{\partial H} \right)_{H=0} = - \left( \frac{\partial^2 G}{\partial H^2} \right)_{H=0}.
$$

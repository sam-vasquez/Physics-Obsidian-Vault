Much like the analysis of the Poincare transformations, we can understand the conformal transformations by considering the action of transformations infinitesimally close to the identity, parameterized by some small $\epsilon$. This gives us a definition of the vector fields on Minkowski space whose flows generate conformal transformations, as the vector fields $\epsilon^\mu$ that satisfy the Conformal Killing Equation.

This equation has a finite number of solutions for spacetime manifolds with dimension greater than 3. See solutions here.

But in 2 spacetime dimensions, there are infinitely many solutions. See solutions here.

These infinitesimal solutions implement the finite conformal transformations on Minkowski space according to their flows. See a derivation of the finite conformal transformations here. (In 2D).
